# 017 - Pointer-reactive gallery
- **Source:** awwwards.com element pattern, common on studio sites (observed 2026-06)
- **Tags:** archetype: studio · domain: interaction · mood: immersive · cost: M
- **What:** An image gallery or grid that pans, tilts, or reveals based on cursor position rather than clicks, making the viewport feel like a window onto a larger surface.
- **Use when:** desktop-heavy creative audience and imagery worth the theater. **Skip when:** mobile-majority traffic; the move vanishes on touch and needs a designed fallback, not an accidental one.
- **Why it works:** cursor reactivity is the cheapest this-site-is-alive signal, rewarding idle exploration without demanding commitment.
- **Build owner:** motion-direction gates it (pointer vocabulary); marketing-page-layout composes the gallery.
- **Do not copy:** attaching it to critical content; pointer effects must be decorative-only.
- **Cost:** M (2 to 6h)
