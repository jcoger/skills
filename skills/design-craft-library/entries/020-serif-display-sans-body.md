# 020 - Serif display over sans body
- **Source:** paraform.com (observed 2026-06)
- **Tags:** archetype: saas · domain: type · mood: editorial · cost: S
- **What:** A serif display face for headlines and pull-quotes set against a sans-serif body, inside a utilitarian B2B/SaaS category. The serif carries the taste and emotion; the sans keeps the body and UI legible.
- **Use when:** a grounded category (recruiting, fintech, ops) that wants to feel considered and premium without going fully editorial. **Skip when:** the product is playful or consumer (the serif reads stuffy), or a second display face can't be licensed/justified.
- **Why it works:** editorial type personality imported into a grounded category; the serif/sans contrast is the signature moment with no motion or imagery required. Reads as restraint and taste. Adjacent to 015 (boring category elevation) but operates purely at the type layer.
- **Build owner:** plain build (type system / brand layer).
- **Do not copy:** the specific serif. Pick a face that fits the brand, not theirs.
- **Cost:** S
