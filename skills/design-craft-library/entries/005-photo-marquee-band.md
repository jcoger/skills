# 005 - Photo marquee band
- **Source:** awwwards.com element pattern, common on DTC sites (observed 2026-06)
- **Tags:** archetype: dtc · domain: imagery · mood: kinetic · cost: S
- **What:** A thin, auto-scrolling, edge-to-edge band of photos between sections, often two rows moving in opposite directions.
- **Use when:** plenty of real photography or social content exists and the page needs a low-cost energy break between dense sections. **Skip when:** fewer than 8 strong images; visible repeats break the spell.
- **Why it works:** adds motion and proof density at rail-shape cost, working as a palate cleanser in the page's vertical rhythm.
- **Build owner:** marketing-page-layout (rail sections).
- **Do not copy:** speeds that fight readability; slow drift, and pause on hover.
- **Cost:** S (under 2h)
