# 009 - Proof wall
- **Source:** common on consumer DTC and app sites (observed 2026-06)
- **Tags:** archetype: dtc · domain: proof · mood: playful · cost: M
- **What:** A dense masonry wall of mixed-format proof: post screenshots, short quotes, star ratings, UGC stills, deliberately unpolished.
- **Use when:** real customer love exists in volume and the audience is consumer. **Skip when:** fewer than about 12 real items (faked density reads instantly) or enterprise buyers who expect logos and case studies.
- **Why it works:** density itself is the argument, and imperfection signals an authenticity polished testimonial cards cannot.
- **Build owner:** marketing-page-layout; the conversion skill decides placement.
- **Do not copy:** fabricated or paraphrased posts; every item must be real.
- **Cost:** M (2 to 6h)
