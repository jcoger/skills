# 008 - Annotated product hotspots
- **Source:** common on premium DTC product pages (observed 2026-06)
- **Tags:** archetype: dtc · domain: imagery · mood: grounded · cost: M
- **What:** One large product image with numbered callouts pointing at exact physical or UI details, each with a line of copy anchored to the point.
- **Use when:** differentiation lives in details a buyer would not notice unaided. **Skip when:** the claims are abstract outcomes rather than observable features.
- **Why it works:** forces copy specificity and rewards close looking; pointing at the product reads as confidence in the product.
- **Build owner:** marketing-page-layout.
- **Do not copy:** more than 6 hotspots; past that it becomes an engineering diagram.
- **Cost:** M (2 to 6h)
