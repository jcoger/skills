# 015 - Boring category elevation
- **Source:** shepherdinsurance.com (observed 2026-06)
- **Tags:** archetype: b2b-services · domain: art-direction · mood: editorial · cost: M
- **What:** Importing editorial-brand codes (serif display type, photographic art direction, generous space) into a commodity category where every competitor looks like brochureware.
- **Use when:** the category's visual baseline is low and the buyer is a human, not a procurement checklist. **Skip when:** the category's visual conventions are themselves trust signals buyers expect.
- **Why it works:** category-relative contrast is cheaper than absolute originality. Looking like a fashion brand in an insurance category costs nothing extra and reframes the company as the obvious modern choice.
- **Build owner:** marketing-page-layout (visual-craft layer).
- **Do not copy:** dropping the category's required trust content (credentials, specifics) for aesthetics.
- **Cost:** M (2 to 6h)
