# 019 - UI-fragment collage over environment photo
- **Source:** paraform.com (observed 2026-06)
- **Tags:** archetype: saas · domain: imagery · mood: immersive · cost: L
- **What:** The product visual is not a full screenshot but a few UI fragments (a contact list, a bar chart, stat tiles, an animated counter) floating in a glassy rounded frame over a soft, blurred lifestyle photo. The interface reads as alive in the world rather than a flat capture.
- **Use when:** the product's value is legible in small UI fragments (lists, charts, counts) and you want warmth a bare screenshot lacks. **Skip when:** the product needs full-context screens to be understood, or you can't afford the bespoke composite + per-state animation.
- **Why it works:** an imagery move that blends product-as-hero with environmental photography; the blur and glass give depth, the floating fragments give focus and motion. Warmer than a flat UI shot, more credible than pure lifestyle. Pairs naturally with a sticky/stack shape.
- **Build owner:** marketing-page-layout + motion-direction.
- **Do not copy:** the volume of bespoke fragment + animation work per state: a large production budget most pages can't match.
- **Cost:** L
