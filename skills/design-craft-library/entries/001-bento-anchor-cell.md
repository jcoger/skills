# 001 - Bento anchor cell
- **Source:** linear.app (observed 2026-06)
- **Tags:** archetype: saas · domain: layout · mood: grounded · cost: M
- **What:** A bento grid where one cell is 2 to 3 times larger than the rest and carries the section's single most important claim or visual. The remaining cells support it instead of competing.
- **Use when:** a feature section has one hero capability and 4 to 6 secondary ones. **Skip when:** all features are equal weight (use a uniform grid) or there are fewer than 4 items.
- **Why it works:** gives the bento shape a focal hierarchy, so the section reads as an argument instead of an inventory. The anchor cell doubles as the section's signature moment.
- **Build owner:** marketing-page-layout (bento sections).
- **Do not copy:** the dark-glass UI skin; the move is the cell hierarchy, not the styling.
- **Cost:** M (2 to 6h)
