# 003 - Kinetic type hero
- **Source:** cash.app (observed 2026-06)
- **Tags:** archetype: fintech · domain: type · mood: kinetic · cost: M
- **What:** The hero headline set at display scale with per-word or per-letter motion: staggered rises, weight shifts, or swapping words. Type IS the hero image.
- **Use when:** the brand voice is loud, no strong product visual exists, and the audience skews consumer. **Skip when:** trust-first B2B audiences, or headlines longer than about 6 words.
- **Why it works:** claims the most valuable viewport with brand energy instead of a stock visual, and doubles as the page's signature motion moment by default.
- **Build owner:** marketing-page-layout composes it; motion-direction owns the timing values.
- **Do not copy:** durations and easings wholesale; pull them from the project's approved motion personality.
- **Cost:** M (2 to 6h)
