# 013 - Anti-design collision
- **Source:** ugly.cash (observed 2026-06)
- **Tags:** archetype: fintech · domain: art-direction · mood: playful · cost: M
- **What:** Deliberate rule-breaking applied as a consistent system: clashing colors, raw 1px borders, sticker graphics, default-looking type, harsh shadows.
- **Use when:** young consumer audience in a crowded category where polish is table stakes, and the brand can commit fully. **Skip when:** trust-sensitive purchases, or any halfway commitment; half-applied anti-design reads as a mistake.
- **Why it works:** in a feed of identical gradient-polish sites, ugliness-on-purpose is the differentiation. Consistency is the entire difference between anti-design and bad design.
- **Build owner:** marketing-page-layout (visual-craft layer); usually pairs with the playful motion personality.
- **Do not copy:** applying it to checkout or trust surfaces; those stay conventional.
- **Cost:** M (2 to 6h)
