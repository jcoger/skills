# 010 - Product UI as hero
- **Source:** flighty.com (observed 2026-06); also seen: linear.app
- **Tags:** archetype: app · domain: imagery · mood: grounded · cost: M
- **What:** The hero visual is the actual product interface, lit and composed like product photography: realistic shadows, device context, one state chosen to tell the whole story.
- **Use when:** the UI is genuinely strong and a single screen can carry the value proposition. **Skip when:** the product is visually weak or back-endy; use a diagrammed abstraction instead.
- **Why it works:** replaces marketing abstraction with evidence. The screenshot is the claim.
- **Build owner:** marketing-page-layout.
- **Do not copy:** multi-screen collages; one screen, one story.
- **Cost:** M (2 to 6h)
