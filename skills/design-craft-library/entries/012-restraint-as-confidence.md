# 012 - Restraint as confidence
- **Source:** paraform.com (observed 2026-06)
- **Tags:** archetype: saas · domain: layout · mood: grounded · cost: S
- **What:** Aggressive whitespace, narrow text measures (44 to 60ch), few elements per viewport, and zero decorative filler: the layout's emptiness is the design statement.
- **Use when:** premium positioning with strong typography and copy that can carry weight. **Skip when:** the offer needs heavy explanation or proof density; for unknown brands, restraint can read as vagueness.
- **Why it works:** white space is read as a price signal. Restraint implies the product does not need to shout.
- **Build owner:** marketing-page-layout (sizing system posture).
- **Do not copy:** the emptiness without the typographic quality; restraint exposes weak type instantly.
- **Cost:** S (under 2h)
