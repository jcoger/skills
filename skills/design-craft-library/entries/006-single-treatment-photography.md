# 006 - Single-treatment photography
- **Source:** functionhealth.com (observed 2026-06)
- **Tags:** archetype: health · domain: imagery · mood: editorial · cost: M
- **What:** Every photo on the site passes through one identical treatment: same grade, same crop logic, same casting mood, so images from different sources read as one shoot.
- **Use when:** imagery comes from mixed sources (stock, product, UGC) and the brand needs editorial authority. **Skip when:** product photography is already a strong, consistent asset.
- **Why it works:** photographic consistency reads as brand confidence; it is the cheapest path to premium with imperfect assets.
- **Build owner:** marketing-page-layout (visual-craft layer).
- **Do not copy:** the specific color grade; derive it from the project palette.
- **Cost:** M (2 to 6h)
