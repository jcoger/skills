# 011 - Oversized stat band
- **Source:** shepherdinsurance.com (observed 2026-06)
- **Tags:** archetype: b2b-services · domain: proof · mood: grounded · cost: S
- **What:** A full-width band carrying one number set at display scale, the largest type on the page after the hero, with a single supporting line.
- **Use when:** one metric is genuinely impressive and verifiable. **Skip when:** numbers are unverifiable or merely decent; a weak stat at large scale damages trust.
- **Why it works:** scale assigns importance. One huge number is more credible than four medium ones, and the band shape resets the page rhythm.
- **Build owner:** marketing-page-layout; the conversion skill picks the stat.
- **Do not copy:** stacking several stat bands on one page; one per page.
- **Cost:** S (under 2h)
