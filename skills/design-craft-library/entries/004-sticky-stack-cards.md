# 004 - Sticky stack cards
- **Source:** awwwards.com element pattern, multiple sites (observed 2026-06)
- **Tags:** archetype: saas · domain: interaction · mood: immersive · cost: M
- **What:** A series of full-width cards that pin and stack as the user scrolls, each new card sliding over the previous one.
- **Use when:** 3 to 5 sequential ideas of equal weight (process steps, value pillars) deserve focused attention one at a time. **Skip when:** content is scannable list material, or there are more than 5 cards.
- **Why it works:** converts a list into a paced reveal; the pin gives each idea a beat of full attention, turning a sticky shape into rhythm.
- **Build owner:** marketing-page-layout composes; motion-direction gates the pin (max 1 pinned section per page).
- **Do not copy:** stacks beyond 5 cards; patience runs out before the cards do.
- **Cost:** M (2 to 6h)
