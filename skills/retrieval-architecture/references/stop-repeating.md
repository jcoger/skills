# Stop Repeating

**Last verified 2026-08-07.** Claims that circulate widely and do not
survive checking. Grep any AEO artifact against this list before it ships.

---

## Debunked

| Claim | What is actually true |
|---|---|
| **"llms.txt improves AI visibility"** | 97% of llms.txt files received **zero requests** in a full month across 137,210 domains. A second study of ~300,000 domains found removing it **improved** their citation-prediction model. Google states you do not need AI text files. No engine has committed to reading it. **Score it zero.** |
| **"Google's March 2026 scaled-content update cost sites 87% of traffic"** | **There was no March 2026 scaled-content update.** A Spam Update ran March 24 (19.5 hours) and a Core Update ran March 27 to April 8. Neither was named or framed as scaled-content policy. The 87% figure attaches to neither. This claim reached a live client proposal in this workspace before it was caught. |
| **"FAQ schema is the highest-ROI AEO asset"** | Q&A format is the only content genre measured with a **negative** effect (−5.74%), and a separate corpus found removing generic FAQ schema improved citation share +7%. Evidence is thin in both directions. **Stop asserting it as an asset.** |
| **"AI referral traffic converts 4.4x better than organic"** | Traces to one vendor blog with no primary dataset. |
| **"Dataset schema lifts citations 30%+"** | Vendor marketing. At least one controlled study found no uplift from schema alone, and none of the highest-performing comparison pages examined used schema at all. |
| **"Google confirmed GBP feeds AI Overviews"** | Google confirmed a **business-owner** Gemini feature for analyzing performance and drafting review replies. That is not documentation that Google Business Profile grounds searcher-facing AI Overviews. Say "observable behavior suggests." |
| **"Vercel went from <1% to 10% of signups from ChatGPT"** | Traces to a single tweet. No dated post, no data. |
| **"Google/AI citation overlap fell from 70% to under 20%"** | Circulates everywhere, links back to nothing. Real overlap figures exist and are in the ledger. Use those. |
| **"67% of pSEO initiatives fail"** and **"93% of penalized sites lacked differentiation"** | No methodology published anywhere for either. |
| **"ChatGPT search is a fine-tuned GPT-4o distilled from o1-preview"** | True as of October 2024 and **stale by the 2026-08 verification**. Search now runs on the GPT-5.x family. Still the most-quoted architecture line in the AEO field. |

## Stale, use with a date

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| Princeton GEO effect sizes (+40% cite sources, +30% quotations) | 2023-11, measured on a **simulated** generative engine against 2023-era models, and it measures **citation given retrieval**, not retrieval. Only the statistics finding has been corroborated on live engines. |
| XFunnel "product content 46 to 70% of citations" | Unreplicated. A later study puts vendor sites at 14.7%. |
| Vercel crawler JavaScript study | 2024-12. Still the most-cited source and **20 months old**. No 2026 replication found. Verify before betting architecture on it. |
| SE Ranking AI Mode volatility data | Collected pre-Gemini-3. Gemini 3 became the AI Overviews default 2026-01-27, which invalidates most pre-2026 citation studies. |
| Cloudflare crawl-to-refer ratios | Secondary sources disagree by more than 2×. Verify at the source. |

## Structural cautions

**Most of the GEO literature is vendor preprints.** A large share of
2026 arXiv GEO papers are single-author submissions from companies selling
visibility products, with no released dataset. Several cluster in a narrow
ID range. Grade before citing.

**Genuinely peer-reviewed work in this field is narrow.** As of August 2026
that is roughly: EMNLP 2025 on cognitive-bias manipulation, SIGIR 2026 on
pretraining exposure, ICWSM 2026 on Google AI Overviews, TMLR 2026 on
inference non-determinism, ICLR 2025 on dark patterns, and MTRAG on
multi-turn retrieval. Everything else is preprint or vendor.

**Nobody has tested whether third-party directory presence causes
citation.** Correlation is documented. Causation has never been tested by
any party. Anyone selling you a confident answer is extrapolating.

**Nobody has published crawl-to-citation latency** for any engine.

**Western-engine studies do not measure cited-source age.** The only
half-life data is from Chinese platforms. Label it if you use it.
